SPOTTER Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

SPOTTER ("we", "the app") is a strength-training tracker. This policy explains what information the app handles and your choices. We do not sell your data and do not show ads or track you across other apps or websites.

Information we collect

  • Training data you enter: workouts, sets, weights, reps, routines, custom exercises, personal records, goals, schedule and settings. By default this is stored only on your device.
  • Profile information: your name or username, avatar/initials, unit preference and training goals, as you choose to provide them.
  • Account information (optional): if you create an account, we store your email address and a secure password hash, or the identifier provided by Sign in with Apple or Google. Apple may give us a private relay email address if you choose to hide your email.
  • Cloud-synced data (only with an account): your training data and profile are backed up to our database so you can use them on other devices.
  • Purchase information: if you subscribe, our subscription provider receives an anonymous app user ID and your subscription status and transaction records from Apple. We never see your Apple ID or payment details.

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, photos, advertising identifier or health-sensor data.

How we use information

  • To provide the app: tracking workouts, calculating stats and personal records, syncing between devices.
  • To authenticate you and secure your account.
  • To manage your subscription and free trial.
  • To respond to support requests.

Service providers

We use these providers to run the app. They process data only on our behalf:

  • Supabase – authentication and database hosting for accounts and sync.
  • RevenueCat – subscription and entitlement management.
  • Apple (App Store, Sign in with Apple) and Google (Sign in with Google) – purchases and sign-in.

Sharing

We share data only with the providers above, when required by law, or to protect our rights and users. Any social or competition features that show other users only display information you choose to share.

Data retention and deletion

Local data stays on your device until you delete the app or use Settings → Erase all local data. If you have an account, you can permanently delete it and all associated cloud data at any time in the app: Profile → Settings → Account & sync → Delete account. You can also contact us (see below) to request deletion.

Subscriptions

SPOTTER Pro is an auto-renewing subscription with a 7-day free trial. Payment is charged to your Apple ID account. You can manage or cancel it at any time in your Apple ID subscription settings (Settings → your name → Subscriptions) or via Settings → Manage / cancel subscription in the app.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit, and cloud data is protected by per-user access rules. No method of storage is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

Children

SPOTTER is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK or California), you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your data and to object to processing. Contact us and we will respond within a reasonable time.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do. Continued use of the app means you accept the updated policy.

Contact

Questions or requests: open an issue at github.com/lucaswhittlebusiness-dev/spotter-legal/issues
