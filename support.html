SPOTTER Support

Need help with SPOTTER? Open an issue and we'll get back to you: github.com/lucaswhittlebusiness-dev/spotter-legal/issues

Common questions

How do I cancel my subscription? In the app go to Profile → Settings → Manage / cancel subscription, or open Settings on your iPhone → your name → Subscriptions → SPOTTER.

How do I restore my purchase? Open the paywall and tap Restore purchases while signed in with the same Apple ID.

How do I delete my account and data? Profile → Settings → Account & sync → Delete account. This permanently removes your account and cloud data.

I forgot my password. On the sign-in screen tap Forgot password and follow the email link on your iPhone.
